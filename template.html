What it checks

It fixes spacing, and capitalization in names and cities written all in capitals or all in lower case. It puts phone numbers in the form 519-555-0142, postal codes in the form A1A 1A1, dates in the form 2025-03-04 (English or French month names), and provinces in whichever style the column mostly uses. It flags empty required cells, invalid email addresses and phone numbers, postal codes that can't be Canadian, and dates that could be read two ways or don't exist. Possible duplicates are listed for a person to confirm, never merged on their own.

What this would look like for your business

A version built for you would start from the export your system already produces, such as a point-of-sale, booking or accounting system, and apply your rules: which fields are required, how names and addresses should look, and what counts as a duplicate. It can run with one click or on a schedule, and it keeps the same change log so anyone can check what changed and why.

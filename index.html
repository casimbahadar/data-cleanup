Clean up a customer list

Load a customer spreadsheet saved as CSV. The tool fixes spacing and capitalization, puts phone numbers, postal codes and dates into one format, flags missing information, and lists possible duplicates for you to check. Every change is logged cell by cell.

Your file stays on this device. This page reads and cleans it in your browser, and its security settings block it from sending your data anywhere.

What this would look like for your business

A version built for you would start from the export your system already produces, such as a point-of-sale, booking or accounting system, and apply your rules: which fields are required, how names and addresses should look, and what counts as a duplicate. It can run with one click or on a schedule, and it keeps the same change log so anyone can check what changed and why.
